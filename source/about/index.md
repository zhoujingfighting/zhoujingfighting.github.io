## Who am I

Hello! My name is **Jing Zhou**, and I am a **Senior LLVM GPGPU Compiler Engineer** based in Hangzhou, China. My research and engineering focus lies in the intersections of **LLVM compiler infrastructure, GPGPU backend code generation, and heterogeneous accelerated computing**.

Before my current role, I spent several years working on LLVM compiler optimization passes and high-performance compilation pipelines.

---

## 🛠 Technical Focus (技术领域)

- **Compiler Technology**: Deep dive into LLVM/Clang infrastructure, custom optimization passes, Intermediate Representation (IR) analysis, and backend instruction scheduling / register allocation.
- **Heterogeneous Computing**: GPGPU compiler toolchains, ROCm (HIP & HSA runtime) compilation flow, CUDA / HIP programming, and kernel descriptor generation.
- **System & Performance**: Profiling GPU kernels, optimizing compute-bound and memory-bound workloads, parallel algorithms, and Linux system-level programming.
- **Infrastructure**: Building robust and reproducible development environments using Docker, devcontainers, and custom toolchains.
